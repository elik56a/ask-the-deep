# Profile

## Who Eliyahu is

Eliyahu Kriel is a Senior Full Stack Engineer with more than 7 years of experience. He works across React, React Native, Node.js and Python, with a focus on monorepo architecture and AWS-based cloud delivery. He currently works at Phytech, an agriculture technology company, where he has been a Senior Full Stack Engineer since 2025.

## What Eliyahu is known for

Eliyahu is known for raising engineering standards through infrastructure. At Phytech he cut asset delivery latency by 40% and made build pipelines 25% faster. He builds team-wide AI developer platforms, using Claude Code, Cursor, custom hooks and rules, that automate quality enforcement, targeted testing and code reuse.

## Eliyahu's career path

Eliyahu started as a Full Stack Engineer at Givers (2019–2020), building real-time telemedicine features. He then spent two years at 019 Mobile (2020–2022), modernizing a legacy PHP system into a Vue.js app and scaling an SMS campaign engine. He joined Phytech in 2022 as a Full Stack Engineer and became a Senior Full Stack Engineer in 2025. From 2023 to 2025 he also served in IDF reserve duty as a Full Stack Engineer in the Lotem / Shahar unit. Across these roles he moved from building features to owning the infrastructure, tooling and quality bar that a whole team builds on.

## Eliyahu's engineering approach

Eliyahu prefers to solve a problem once, in a shared place: a shared package for web and mobile, one publish pipeline for every package, one set of rules enforced by tooling instead of by memory. He writes tests as part of the work, not after it, and lets automation enforce the standards, so code review can focus on design. He works in small, clearly named commits and designs larger features in milestones, starting from the API contract and the data.

## Eliyahu's current focus

Eliyahu is moving deeper into AI engineering and backend work. At Phytech he builds AI developer tooling on Claude Code and added multi-provider AI support (OpenAI, Anthropic and OpenRouter) to a Java service with LangChain4j. On his own he built this portfolio's chat agent in Python, with FastAPI, Pydantic AI and retrieval over a knowledge base, to practice building AI systems the way production systems are built: with evaluations, cost limits and observability.
