# 0019. Dive v3: paced dive, the answer at the seabed, one type family

Date: 2026-10-22 · Status: accepted · Amends 0010

## Context

ADR 0010 built the sea dive from the first prototype. Two more prototype rounds were tested and approved as artifacts: [v2](https://claude.ai/artifact/VLpm612BxMQKPTzkJaTv3e) and [v3](https://claude.ai/artifact/Qw56GwMHcf9HEgjP4N823X). v3 is the new reference for look and behaviour. What they showed:

- A live run is over in 2–3 s, too fast to follow. The process only reads as a journey when it is paced.
- With the chat beside the log, people read the answer and skip the dive. When the answer waits at the seabed, they watch it.
- Starting the dive straight away feels abrupt. A short pre-dive check sets the scene and says what will happen (nothing stored, the pace).
- Instrument Serif next to Heebo and JetBrains Mono looked like three ideas. One family reads as one product.
- Source chips that only open a local passage hide where the text lives. The knowledge files are public in git since ADR 0018, so a source can link to its exact section.
- Five models leave the deep zones empty.

## Decision

- **Type.** Archivo (variable width and weight) is the only text family. Headings use it wide, like the markings on dive gear, and UI text uses it at normal width. IBM Plex Mono is for numbers and labels only. Heebo stays as the Hebrew fallback. All three are self-hosted with `@fontsource`. Instrument Serif and JetBrains Mono are removed.
- **Paced playback.** A dive plays the real events at about 30 s per question, with Pause, 2× and Skip. Times shown in the HUD and receipt stay the server's real times.
- **The answer waits for the seabed.** The chat leaves during the dive. The current step is large, and its text streams in word by word. The chat comes back after the seabed report.
- **Pre-dive check.** Every dive starts behind a veil: a dive-watch gauge, a few checks, then a short countdown. Descend skips it; Not now or Esc cancels.
- **One dive per question.** Each answer can replay its dive. A follow-up shows the earlier messages it carries. "New conversation" clears the thread (still browser-only, ADR 0007).
- **At the seabed** the chat sits at the bottom centre over a lit seabed. Rereading the log folds it into an "Open chat" pill that opens a compact side panel. On mobile it is a bottom sheet.
- **Sources link to GitHub**, to the knowledge file at the passage's heading anchor, with the passage shown on hover and focus.
- **A fuller sea: 24 CC BY / CC0 models** placed by depth (v3 added the seabed props; the statue was dropped, and the Ginnanmaru wreck too, as it barely showed in the dark). Compressed with meshopt and WebP they come to 8 MB, 1.4 MB before the first question; meshopt decodes in WebAssembly, so the CSP allows `'wasm-unsafe-eval'`. Budget: at most 3 MB of models before the first question (the surface and the diver), at most 15 MB in total, loaded zone by zone while diving.

## Consequences

- An answer takes about 30 s to reach unless the visitor skips. "Stay at the surface" (ADR 0010) and Skip keep the fast path.
- The page weighs more. It stays usable before any model loads, and the scene still works without them.
- Source links depend on GitHub heading anchors matching passage ids, so a test guards that.
- We would revisit pacing if analytics show most visitors skip, and the model budget if the dive drops frames on mid-range phones.
